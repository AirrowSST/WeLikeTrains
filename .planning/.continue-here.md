Stage 0 corrected the current DataMall paths and added schema-specific parsers:

- `PubFloodAlerts`
- `v4/TrafficSpeedBands`
- `EstTravelTimes`

Traffic-speed records retain link/road metadata, segment endpoints, speed band
and minimum/maximum speed. Expressway records retain direction, section,
destination and estimated minutes. PUB records retain CAP-style identity,
headline, description/instruction, severity, area, point and expiry.

These inputs are intentionally informational. They have zero additive delay,
and the planner skips zero-delay traffic records so they cannot falsely change
journey ranking, uncertainty or the affected-route presentation before proper
route matching exists.

The local app can expose feed health under **Disruptions → Know what you're
looking at** after calculating a live plan. Parsed records are visible in the
`/api/plan` response under `conditions.traffic` and `conditions.notices`. The
deployed Cloud Run app has not been updated.

## Critical Anti-Patterns (do NOT repeat these)

- Do not add a fixed delay merely because a road-speed or section-time record
  exists. First establish route intersection, baseline semantics and bounds.
- Do not infer stable bus trip/vehicle identity from loose coordinate matching;
  DataMall BusArrival does not expose the same identity as GTFS Realtime.
- Do not download GTFS at runtime, test startup or every request.
- Do not commit `.env`, `.local`, `.cache`, tokens or provider payload dumps.
- Do not touch or commit unrelated dirty files. `docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md` is a
  mixed-ownership file; isolate any future hunk carefully.
- Do not deploy without a fresh explicit request from the user.
